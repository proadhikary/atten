Leave policy

Members earn this many days every month and unused days carry forward within the cycle. Requests beyond the balance are still allowed — they just arrive flagged.

Menstrual days are an extra grant for members marked female, on top of the rate above, and can only be spent on a leave request of type Menstrual — so those members see {{ (leave_rate + menstrual_leave_rate)|days }} day(s) a month. Lab network

Discord notifications

Post a message to a Discord channel every time someone checks in or out. Create the webhook in Discord under Channel → Edit Channel → Integrations → Webhooks.

Treat this like a password — anyone holding it can post to your channel. Leave blank to turn notifications off.
