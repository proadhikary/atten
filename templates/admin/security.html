How proxy attendance is caught

Web browsers cannot read a device's MAC address — no website can. Instead, each account is bound to at most {{ device_limit }} devices: the first check-in registers the browser, and a third one is refused. If an account marks attendance from a device already registered to someone else, the attempt is blocked, that day's attendance is voided, and a flag is raised here for you to review.
